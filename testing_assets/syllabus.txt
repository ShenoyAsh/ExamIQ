Unit 1: Calculus
- Differentiation: Power rule, product rule, quotient rule.
- Integration: Indefinite and definite integrals, integration by parts.
- Applications of derivatives: Maxima and Minima.

Unit 2: Algebra
- Quadratic Equations: Finding roots, discriminant.
- Complex Numbers: Polar form, De Moivre's Theorem.
- Matrices and Determinants: Inverse, Cramer's rule.

Unit 3: Statistics and Probability
- Probability Distributions: Binomial, Poisson, Normal.
- Correlation and Regression.
- Mean, Median, Mode for grouped data.

Unit 4: Geometry
- Coordinate Geometry: Straight lines, circles.
- Vectors in 3D space.
