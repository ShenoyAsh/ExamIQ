Section A (Calculus)
1. Find the derivative of f(x) = x^3 + 5x^2 - 7x + 10.
2. Evaluate the integral of sin(x) from 0 to pi.
3. Determine the maximum value of the function y = -2x^2 + 8x + 5.

Section B (Algebra)
4. Solve the quadratic equation 3x^2 - 12x + 9 = 0.
5. Find the inverse of the matrix A = [[1, 2], [3, 4]].
6. If z = 1 + i, express z in polar form.

Section C (Statistics)
7. A bag contains 5 red and 3 blue balls. Find the probability of picking 2 red balls.
8. Calculate the standard deviation for the set: {10, 12, 14, 16, 18}.
